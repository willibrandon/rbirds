Live frame pacing on an Apple M4 Pro (12 cores: 8 performance, 4
efficiency; macOS 26.5.2), 2026-09-26 UTC. rbirds 0.1.1 release build with
big-flock mode (D-006) and the deadline sleep (D-007). "rbirds before" is
the same version without them (commit 2fa8167). C: the pinned cbirds built
with Apple clang 17.0.0, -std=c99 -O3 -g. All runs --color ember --seed 1.

tools/pacing.py: each program live under a pseudoterminal for 10 s, output
read as fast as it comes, device status requests answered as a terminal
does. Gaps are between the starts of successive frames, leaving out the
first second. "cores" is the program's CPU time over the run's wall time.

The host was also running other work (load average 3 to 6). In 30 s runs
side by side, cbirds had 2 and 20 gaps over 25 ms and rbirds 1 and 1; the
longest gap in each was 26 to 39 ms, for both. An earlier version of the
harness could answer a request twice when a read ended on it; these runs
are with the fixed one.

tools/vhs/stalls.sh: each command live in VHS 0.12.1's terminal (ttyd and
xterm.js in headless Chrome), 1400x800, recorded at 25 frames a second. A
still is a video frame that differs from the one before by less than 8 in
every pixel; only 4 s to 12 s of the flight counts, so the intro and the
flight out after q are left out.

Repeated at 65536 birds while the host was busy (load average up to 12):
two of four runs had one video frame the same as the one before (39 ms),
and none two in a row.

What the design was chosen from, measured the same way in VHS (longest
still between 4 s and 12 s, capture rate in brackets when it fell):

  every core, no pacing, birds drawn smaller as the flock grew:
    30000 birds (11 px)   80 ms       65536 birds (7 px)   709 ms (19.8 fps)
  one thread, no pacing:
    8192, 16384, 32768    none
  no pacing, 30 px birds:        4 threads   6 threads   12 threads
    16384                        none        none        none
    32768                        none        none        159 ms
    65536                        none        550 ms (23.6 fps)   650 ms (20.0 fps)
  pacing, 30 px birds:
    32768                        none        none        none
    65536                        none        none        565 ms (23.0 fps)

Without pacing, a terminal that falls behind the frames shows it as a
still, and on more threads it also runs short of CPU (the capture rate fell
with it). Pacing keeps the terminal at most a frame behind, and half the
cores leaves it the CPU. Drawing birds smaller more than doubled the bytes a
frame (97 KB against 43 KB at 65536 birds, --bench) and was dropped.

Sleep overshoot on the same Mac (std::thread::sleep, 60 sleeps each):
  2 ms: 0.95 ms late    7 ms: 1.96    10 ms: 3.19    14 ms: 5.08    16 ms: 4.96 (medians)
  slept towards a 7 ms or 14 ms deadline a millisecond at a time: 0.45 and 0.21 ms late
